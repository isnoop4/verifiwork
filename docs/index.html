VerifiWork

AI-judged escrow for gig data-work — quality verified against a public rubric, payment released automatically.

GenLayer Agent Tank · Future of Work

The Problem

Gig platform data-workers — transcription, AI evaluation, annotation — face subjective quality reviews from a single reviewer, slow and opaque payment disputes, and reputation that's locked into a single platform.

How It Works

1

Requester posts a task with a reward and a public quality rubric. Funds go into on-chain escrow.

2

Worker submits their work.

3

AI validator committee judges the submission against the rubric using GenLayer's Equivalence Principle.

4

Consensus verdict releases funds automatically — no manual review, no single point of judgment.

Tested End-to-End

Rejected

A weak, incomplete transcript is correctly rejected. Funds are refunded to the requester automatically.

Approved

A coherent, well-formed transcript is correctly approved. Funds are released to the worker automatically.

Try It

Deploy contracts/transcription_escrow.py on GenLayer Studio, then call create_task → submit_result → evaluate_and_release. Full details in the repository README.
